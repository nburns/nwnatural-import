# NW Natural Import

Pull your NW Natural monthly gas usage and billed cost into Home
Assistant's Energy dashboard.

- Logs into `nwnatural.com/account/gas-usage`.
- Scrapes up to 3 years of monthly meter reads.
- Imports two long-term statistics: consumption (ft³) + cost (USD).

See **DOCS** tab for setup and options.

Source: https://github.com/nburns/nwnatural-import
